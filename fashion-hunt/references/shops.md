# The bookmarked shops

What each bookmarked shop sells, how to search it, its notes file under `references/shops/`, and
how to check its brands and categories as they are today. Checked on 17 November 2026. Brands
come and go, so none is listed as a shop's range: check a shop's brands live, as its entry says.

## Which shops to search

- Skip a shop only when the table below rules the item's kind out (only eBay.de, Vinted,
  Farfetch, Yaga and Care of Carl stock umbrellas, and Vestiaire, YOOX, JoliCloset and ReSee a few; the bag
  shops have no jackets), and
  name it in the report's notes as skipped, with why. Kinds change slowly, but a "few" can run out
  or start: when it matters, check the shop's categories live.
- Never skip a shop for its brands from memory. The scans look the brand up themselves
  (Vestiaire, Farfetch, JoliCloset, Yaga, Vinted, ReSee, A Retro Tale, Collector Square, and the
  Shopify scan by vendor) and say when it has none. An agent searching a shop without a scan
  starts with the shop's live brand check below, and stops there when the brand isn't carried.
- eBay.de and Vinted carry nearly every brand: search them by text as their notes say, with no
  brand check first.
- When a shop's row is unclear for the item, give it to the small-shops agent, which settles it
  with the live check. A shop added to the bookmarks later isn't here: give it to that agent too,
  and add it here afterwards.

## The shops

### Marketplaces

**Vestiaire Collective** (vestiairecollective.com): `scan.ts vestiaire`, vestiaire.md.
- Pre-owned, women's, men's and kids', authenticated on request; fast-fashion brands are banned, so
  it's luxury and premium. Sellers worldwide. Umbrellas only now and then, with no category of their own
  (a Pasotti in November 2026, a vintage Fendi in a hidden "Life & Living" tree); no home goods.
- Brands now: the scan finds the designer by name and stops when there's none. By hand, the
  [A-Z designers page](https://www.vestiairecollective.com/brands/), or the search service's
  `brand` facet for a name (vestiaire.md).

**Vinted** (vinted.ee): `scan.ts vinted`, vinted.md.
- Women, men, kids, a Designer section (`/catalog/2993-designer`), home, electronics and more. New
  with tags is common, not just pre-owned; every brand from high-street to luxury. Private sellers
  across Vinted's countries. Umbrellas: `/catalog/1851-umbrellas` (women's) and
  `/catalog/2981-umbrellas` (men's).
- Brands now: from a vinted.ee tab, `https://api.vinted.ee/svc-filters/filters/search?attribute_ids[catalog]=&attribute_ids[brand]=&attribute_ids[status]=&attribute_ids[color]=&attribute_ids[material]=&filter_search_code=brand&filter_search_text=<name>`
  gives up to 10 `{id, title}` (one brand can have several ids); then `/catalog?brand_ids[]=<id>`
  shows the count, exact below 500.

**eBay.de**: `scan.ts ebay`, ebay.md.
- Everything, new and used, private and business sellers, mostly in Germany and many abroad; only
  Buy It Now counts. Umbrellas: [women's](https://www.ebay.de/b/Damen-Schirme/105569/bn_1598726)
  and [men's](https://www.ebay.de/b/Herren-Schirme/90634/bn_788461), mostly new.
- Brands: search with the brand in the query; on a `/b/` category page `?Marke=<Brand>&mag=1&rt=nc`
  filters and the heading counts.

**Farfetch** (farfetch.com/ee): `scan.ts farfetch`, farfetch.md.
- New from boutiques and brands, women's, men's and kids', with jewellery, watches and a Lifestyle
  (home) section, plus pre-owned from resellers (Louis Vuitton only as "Louis Vuitton Pre-Owned").
  Luxury and contemporary, no high-street. Women's accessories have an Umbrellas filter (new: Moschino,
  Burberry, Barbour, BOSS).
- Brands now: the designers pages for [women](https://www.farfetch.com/ee/designers/women) and
  [men](https://www.farfetch.com/ee/designers/men), pre-owned brands as separate entries ("<Brand>
  Pre-Owned"); a brand's listing is `/ee/shopping/<women|men>/<slug>/items.aspx`. One page every
  10 seconds or slower: it answers bursts with 429 for an hour.

**YOOX** (yoox.com/ee): browser, small-shops.md.
- New, discounted off-season stock from YOOX itself: women's, men's and kids' clothing, shoes,
  bags, small leather goods, belts, eyewear, jewellery and watches, and Home & Design. No umbrella
  category (two Karl Lagerfeld ones were in "Other accessories"). No Louis Vuitton, Hermès, Chanel,
  Prada or Miu Miu, and no high-street mass brands.
- Brands now: the designer index for [women](https://www.yoox.com/ee/designerindex/women),
  [men](https://www.yoox.com/ee/designerindex/men) or [kids](https://www.yoox.com/ee/designerindex/kids),
  one A-Z page; a brand's page shows "N items" (capped at 200+). Categories:
  [women's category index](https://www.yoox.com/ee/categoryindex/women) and the men's and kids' ones.

**JoliCloset** (jolicloset.com): `scan.ts jolicloset`, jolicloset.md.
- Pre-owned, checked in Paris: women's bags, clothing, jewellery and fine watches, shoes and
  accessories (scarves, sunglasses, belts, hats, gloves); a men's department with watches; and a
  Boutique of new pieces. Luxury and premium, some high-street (Zara, Hugo Boss). No home, no kids,
  no umbrella category (a search finds a few).
- Brands now: the [women's designer index](https://www.jolicloset.com/en/designer-index-women);
  `/en/designers-<women|men>/<slug>` shows "N items" (JSON-LD `offerCount`), or "No item found".

**Yaga** (yaga.ee): `scan.ts yaga`, yaga.md.
- Estonian and Latvian sellers, new and pre-owned, every department (home, beauty, kids too);
  mostly high-street with designers in volume. Umbrellas have no category: search the text
  `vihmavari`, since sellers leave their brand blank.
- Brands now: [/api/brand](https://www.yaga.ee/api/brand) and [/api/category](https://www.yaga.ee/api/category);
  `/api/product/search/?brands=[<id>]&offset=0&limit=1` returns an empty list when there's none.

### Smaller shops

**Care of Carl** (careofcarl.com, Borås): browser, small-shops.md.
- New menswear from 250+ premium and luxury brands: clothing, shoes, bags, accessories (belts,
  scarves, hats, gloves, sunglasses, ties, jewellery, wallets, umbrellas at
  [/en/accessories/umbrellas](https://www.careofcarl.com/en/accessories/umbrellas)), watches and
  Lifestyle (home, fragrance). Plus a pre-owned section of women's designer bags, luxury watches and
  some menswear. No women's department.
- Brands now: [/brands](https://www.careofcarl.com/brands); `/en/<brand-slug>` is a 404 when it
  isn't carried, and "show 0 of 0" when it has none in stock. Search `/en/search?q=<term>`.

**ReSee** (resee.com, Paris): `scan.ts resee`, small-shops.md.
- Vintage designer pieces, mostly women's: clothing, shoes, bags, accessories (scarves, belts,
  sunglasses, small leather goods, hats), jewellery, some watches, home objects and books, a small
  men's section. About a sixth ships from the US.
- Brands now: the scan reads its search service; by hand, the
  [designers index](https://www.resee.com/en/designers), where `/en/shop/designers/<slug>` is a 404
  for a brand it has never had.

**Eppli** (eppli.com, Stuttgart): browser, small-shops.md.
- An auction house whose shop sells pre-owned fashion (clothes, fur, bags, shoes, Hermès scarves,
  belts, sunglasses), jewellery, watches, art, interior and collectibles. Search the shop, not the
  auction lots. No umbrellas.
- Brands now: [/en/brands/](https://www.eppli.com/en/brands/); `/en/brands/<slug>/` is a 404 when
  absent, and `/en/brands/<slug>/<category>/` narrows it.

**A Retro Tale** (aretrotale.com, Stockholm): `scan.ts aretrotale`, small-shops.md.
- Pre-owned luxury bags, jewellery, watches and accessories (scarves, wallets, belts, headwear,
  some eyewear). No clothing, shoes, umbrellas or gloves.
- Brands and categories now: the scan reads its search service, whose `brand` and `folder` facets
  count what's in stock.

**Collector Square** (collectorsquare.com, Paris): `scan.ts collectorsquare`, small-shops.md.
- Pre-owned luxury bags (with a few wallets, small leather goods and belts), watches, jewellery,
  and art and collectable objects. No clothing, shoes, scarves, hats, sunglasses or umbrellas.
- Brands now: each department page's Brands panel ([bags](https://www.collectorsquare.com/en/bags.html),
  [watches](https://www.collectorsquare.com/en/watches.html)); `/en/bags/<brand-slug>/` is a 404 when
  absent.

**Relove** (relove.fi, Helsinki): browser, small-shops.md.
- About 50 products online, all second-hand: luxury bags and a few accessories. Its shops sell
  clothing in person, not online.
- Brands now: read its two online category pages; the brand filter counts each.

### The Shopify shops

`scan.ts shopify` reads all nine at once, by vendor, so it tells itself which carry a brand
(shopify.md). For a quick look by hand from a shop's own tab (Bam's paths start with `/en-eu`):
`/search/suggest.json?q=<brand or item>&resources[type]=product&resources[options][unavailable_products]=hide`
lists what's in stock (matching is fuzzy: compare the vendor or title), and `/collections/vendors?q=<Brand>`
is a 404 when no product ever had that vendor. Brand collections and menu counts are no stock test:
some keep sold items, some are empty.

- **Curated by NOAR** (Tallinn): pre-owned designer womenswear (dresses, jackets and coats, knits,
  skirts, trousers), shoes, a few bags, accessories and jewellery. Not luxury only.
- **Labellov** (Antwerp): pre-loved and never-worn luxury: bags, shoes, scarves, jewellery,
  clothing (mostly Chanel), wallets, belts, hats, watches, a little home. Its `/products.json` stops
  at 25,000 of 45,000 products, so use [shop-by-designer](https://labellov.com/collections/shop-by-designer)
  and its brand, category and subcategory filters.
- **Queen Station** (Helsinki): pre-owned and new luxury bags, wallets, jewellery, scarves, belts,
  a few hats, gloves, ties and sunglasses; some men's. [all-products-in-stock](https://queenstation.fi/collections/all-products-in-stock)
  counts brands and types.
- **Designer Exchange** (Dublin): pre-loved bags, wallets, jewellery, sunglasses, scarves, a few
  belts, hats, watches and jackets. [new-arrivals](https://www.designerexchange.ie/collections/new-arrivals)
  holds everything in stock, with brand and type counts.
- **ReLuxe Room** (Sofia): pre-owned luxury bags, wallets, jewellery, a few belts and cases.
  [all-designers](https://www.reluxeroom.com/collections/all-designers) holds what's in stock.
- **Affordable Luxury** (Stockholm): pre-loved luxury bags, jewellery, a few wallets, watches and
  belts. [fivenow, in stock](https://affordableluxurys.com/collections/fivenow?filter.v.availability=1)
  counts brands and types.
- **Luxury Helsinki**: pre-loved luxury bags, jewellery, watches, a few belts and sunglasses.
  [all-products, in stock](https://luxuryhelsinki.fi/collections/all-products?filter.v.availability=1).
- **Bam Luxury Store** (Barcelona): pre-owned luxury bags and wallets, a little jewellery, plus its
  own new organisers. [available](https://bamluxurystore.com/en-eu/collections/available) is in
  stock only; sales are final.
- **Collector's Cage** (Copenhagen): pre-owned luxury bags only, with wallets, straps and charms.
  Its Brands menu, in every page's header, gives each brand's count in stock, as does the vendor
  facet of [/collections/shop](https://collectorscage.com/collections/shop).
